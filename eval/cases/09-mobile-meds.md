Build the "Today" screen of a medication schedule web app for family caregivers who manage pills for an elderly parent. Show today's doses grouped by time of day, each with the drug name, dose, and whether it was given; a way to mark a dose as given or skipped (with an undo); a missed-dose warning; and a refill reminder when a supply drops below 5 days. It must work one-handed at 320px wide and be readable by a tired person at night. No real patient data exists; use clearly sample data.
