Build the sign-up page for a city library's summer reading program for kids aged 6 to 12 and their parents. Kids log the minutes they read and earn a stamp for every 5 hours; the program runs from July 1 to August 31 and ends with a party at the main branch. Single job: a parent registers one or more children (name, age, home branch). Include the weekly events calendar for the three branches, what kids get at each stamp, and an accessible, non-patronizing tone for both kids and parents. Use plausible sample events.
