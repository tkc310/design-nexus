Build the shift-lead view of a warehouse picking dashboard, used on a 24-inch monitor on the floor and occasionally on a tablet. It shows: orders due before the next truck cutoff (with a countdown), pick rate per zone against target, pickers who are idle for more than 10 minutes, and a list of exceptions (short picks, damaged items) that the lead can assign to someone. Primary actions: "Reassign picker" and "Resolve exception". Include loading, a zone with no data, and a lost-connection state. The floor is noisy and bright; glanceability matters more than density.
