Harbor Rowing Club

Learn to row this autumn

Eight Saturday sessions take you from the dock to a steady crew stroke. Boats, oars and a coach are included; bring warm layers and a water bottle.

Mornings

Two crews on the water at 6:30, back by 8 for work or school.

Evenings

Calm water after 18:00 in summer; ergometer sessions in winter.

Weekends

Long rows on Saturday, technique clinic and coffee on Sunday.

Before your first row

Pass the 50m swim check at the city pool, sign the waiver at the boathouse, and arrive 15 minutes early on day one.
