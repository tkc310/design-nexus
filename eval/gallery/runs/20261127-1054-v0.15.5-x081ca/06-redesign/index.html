How a card moves

  1. To do

    Rebuild the signup form

    Write it down

    One line for the job, a label, and a date if it has one. It goes in the first column.

  2. In progress

    Rebuild the signup formSO

    Pick it up

    Whoever takes the job moves the card over. Their initials go on it.

  3. Review

    Rebuild the signup formSO

    Hand it over

    When it's ready for someone else to look at, it waits here, in plain sight.

  4. Done

    Rebuild the signup formSO

    Let it land

    The card reaches the last column, and the team can see it's finished without asking.
