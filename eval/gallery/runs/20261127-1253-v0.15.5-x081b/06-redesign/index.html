What's on the board

  • Four columns

    Each column shows its card count, so a pile-up in Review is plain to see.

  • One card, one task

    A title, a label, a date and the initials of whoever has it. Cards in progress show how far along they are.

  • Filters

    Narrow the board by assignee, due date or label, and group it by status.

  • Projects

    Each project gets its own board, listed down the side with its card count.

How a card moves

  1. 1 Write it down

    Anyone adds a card to To do with a label, a date and a name.

  2. 2 Pass it on

    Move it to In progress when work starts and to Review when someone else needs to look.

  3. 3 Close it

    In Done it stays dated, so the month's finished work is still on the wall.
