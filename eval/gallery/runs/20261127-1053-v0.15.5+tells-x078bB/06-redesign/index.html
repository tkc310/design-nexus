Handoff

A shared board for small teams

Stop asking who’s got it.

Handoff is one board your whole team keeps open. Every task has one owner, one column and one date, so the Monday meeting can be about the work and not about where the work is.

The board

  1. 1

    Filters you can read aloud

    Assignee: anyone. Due: this month. Label: any. Three plain choices narrow the board, and no query language is involved.

  2. 2

    Columns keep count

    Each column says how much is sitting in it. Three in Review and four In progress tells you where the queue is before anyone opens a card.

  3. 3

    One owner per card

    A card carries one set of initials. Not a team, not “everyone”. When it moves to Review, the next person finds it in their inbox.
