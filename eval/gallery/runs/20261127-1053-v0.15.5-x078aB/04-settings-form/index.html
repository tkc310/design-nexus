Prototype controls and assumptions
  • No backend: saving, testing and connecting Slack are simulated with short delays.
  • Changes are saved explicitly. Connecting Slack and sending a test take effect immediately.
  • Weekly digest is not offered as a push notification.
  • Quiet hours hold push and Slack only; an end time earlier than the start runs overnight.
  • Slack choices are kept while Slack is disconnected and apply once it is connected.
