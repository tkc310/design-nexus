Every task sits in one of four columns

  1. To do 5 cards

    Agreed, not started. It already has an owner and a date.

  2. In progress 4 cards

    Someone is on it. The bar under the title shows how far.

  3. Review 3 cards

    Waiting for a second look. Comments stay on the card.

  4. Done 5 cards

    Finished work stays on the board, so the month has a record.
