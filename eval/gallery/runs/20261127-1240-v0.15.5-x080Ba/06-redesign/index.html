How a task moves

To do

Write the task down, give it a label and a due date, and put someone’s initials on it.

In progress

The owner keeps it. A bar on the card shows how far along it is, and the due date turns amber when it’s close.

Review

Comments gather on the card, so whoever checks the work doesn’t have to ask where things stand.

Done

It stays on the board with its date, so the month’s finished work is still in view.
