Prototype controls and assumptions

This is a front-end prototype with no server. Use these to review the failure paths.

  • Preferences are saved with the Save button; connecting Slack and sending a test happen immediately.
  • Slack starts disconnected. Slack choices are kept and take effect once it is connected.
  • The weekly digest is not offered as a push notification.
  • Quiet hours may cross midnight; only an identical start and end is invalid.
