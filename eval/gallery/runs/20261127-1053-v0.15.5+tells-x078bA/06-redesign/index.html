A task board for small teams

See who has what, and what’s waiting.

Tackboard is one shared board for a small team. Four columns, a name on every task, and a date where it matters. Nothing to configure before the first card goes up.

Left to right, the way work actually moves

The counts below are the ones on the board above. Three tasks sitting in Review is the kind of thing you want to notice on Tuesday, not Friday.

  1. To do5

    Write it down, give it to someone

    A task is a sentence, a label and a person. Add a date only if there is one.

  2. In progress4

    A bar for how far along

    Each card carries a thin progress bar, and the date turns amber the day before it’s due.

  3. Review3

    Waiting gets its own column

    Work that needs a second opinion stops hiding inside “in progress”. The comment count sits on the card.

  4. Done5

    Finished, and still findable

    Closed tasks keep their date and their owner, so nobody has to ask whether it went out.
