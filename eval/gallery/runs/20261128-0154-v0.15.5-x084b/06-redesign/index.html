How a week moves

To do

Write the task down with an owner and a date. When the lane gets long, filter it by who, when, or which label.

In progress

A bar under each card shows how far along it is, and a card due tomorrow says so, so nobody has to ask.

Review

Work waiting on someone else's eyes. The comment count is on the front of the card.

Done

Finished cards keep their date, so on Friday you can see what the team actually shipped.
