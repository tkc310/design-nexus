A card's week

A job moves the way it would on the wall: someone writes it, it sits in one person's slot, and when it moves on, the note moves with it.

  1. Mon Ben takes the call

    He writes "Menu proofs, Café Lune" and slots it under Aiko, with the client's quirks on the back.

  2. Tue Aiko pulls it

    Proof 1 goes out. She ticks it off and adds a line asking Marta for the new prices.

  3. Thu Approved by phone

    Aiko passes the card to Marta with one note: "Approved, invoice it." No thread to dig through.

  4. Fri Marta drops it in Done

    The invoice goes out. The card leaves the board on Monday and stays searchable in the drawer.

The whole week on one screen

One column per person
Who is overloaded shows as a tall column, before anyone has to say it in standup.
One card per job
No subtasks of subtasks. If it needs another card, write another card.
Handoffs leave a note
Moving a card asks for one line. The next person reads it on the card, not in a chat.
