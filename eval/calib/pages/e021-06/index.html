How a slip moves

Three steps, the same ones the paper envelope used. Nothing to configure.

  1. 1

    Write the route

    Say what needs doing and list the people it goes through, in order.

  2. 2

    Initial and pass

    When your part is finished, add a one-line note and pass it. Your name is crossed off and the next person is told.

  3. 3

    Close it out

    The last name closes the slip. The whole route stays readable, so anyone can see who said what and when.

Your desk

Holding

Waiting on
Slips you passed that have not moved since. One tap nudges the holder.
Sitting
Any slip held for two working days is shown to whoever started it.
