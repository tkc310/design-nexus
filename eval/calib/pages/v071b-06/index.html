How a slip travels

Three moves, in this order, for every job your team passes around.

  1. 1

    Write the slip

    Name the job, list the people it has to pass through, and give each one a day. That list is the route.

  2. 2

    Hold it

    The slip sits with one person at a time. Their name is circled, so nobody has to ask whose desk it’s on.

  3. 3

    Pass it on

    Done with your part? Cross yourself off. The next name is circled, they get one email, and your dates stay on the trail.

What every slip keeps

No boards to groom and no status meetings. The slip itself answers the three questions a small team asks most.

One holder
A slip is never “with the team”. It is with Ines, since Friday, and it moves the moment she crosses herself off.
A trail that stays
Crossed-off names stay on the slip with the day they passed it on. Changing the route is written on the trail too.
Small-team sized
Made for 3 to 30 people. Every slip fits on one screen, and every route fits on one slip.

The list

Every open slip, whose name is circled on it, and which one is overdue. Open it on Monday instead of asking around.
