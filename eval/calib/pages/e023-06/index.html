Every job knows whose desk it’s on.

Next Desk works like the routing slip on an interoffice envelope. List the people a job passes through, in order. Each one initials it when they’re done, and it moves to the next desk on its own. Nobody has to ask “who has this now?”

We’ll email you a sign-in link. There’s no password to set up.

How a slip moves

No.StepWhat you doWho hears about it
  1. 1

    Write the slip

    One line for the job, a due date, and the file or link it’s about. That’s the whole form.

    Only you, until you route it.

  2. 2

    Route it

    Add the desks in the order the work should travel: design, then copy, then legal. Change the order any time before a desk initials.

    The first desk, by email.

  3. 3

    Initial and pass

    When your part is done, initial the slip. Your name is crossed out, the next desk is circled, and the slip lands in their list.

    The next desk, and everyone already on the slip.

One line per slip, for the whole team

The team view lists every open slip with whose desk it’s on and who gets it next. Sort by waiting time and the stuck ones rise to the top, so the Monday check-in takes a minute instead of a meeting.

  • JonasCircled: on that desk now.
  • RenCrossed out: initialled and passed on.
  • LateStamped: past its due date.
